# Banana Nutritional Profile and Health-Based Mapping

**Basis:** Fresh banana as sold by Tesco. Values are per 100 g from the commercial nutrition panel.

## 1. Nutritional Profile

| Nutrient / Feature | Value per 100 g |
|---|---:|
| Energy | 90 kcal |
| Fat | 0.1 g |
| Saturated fat | 0.1 g |
| Carbohydrate | 20.3 g |
| Total sugars | 18.1 g |
| Dietary fibre | 1.4 g |
| Protein | 1.2 g |
| Salt | <0.01 g |
| Vitamin C | 9 mg |
| Potassium | 330 mg |

**Commercial source:** https://www.tesco.com/groceries/en-GB/products/266420677

## 2. Final Health-Condition Mapping

| Health condition | Result | Main nutritional factors | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Diabetes / blood-glucose management | **Less Favorable** | Carbohydrate, total sugars, dietary fibre, energy | At 20.3 g carbohydrate and 18.1 g sugars per 100 g, the banana has a relatively high carbohydrate/sugar load compared with lower-sugar fruits. It still provides 1.4 g fibre, so the classification is based on the overall profile rather than sugar alone. |
| Hypertension / high blood pressure | **Favorable** | Potassium, salt | The commercial profile reports 330 mg potassium and less than 0.01 g salt per 100 g. Potassium can help counter the effects of sodium and support blood-pressure management. |
| Iron-deficiency anemia | **Neutral** | Iron-related evidence, vitamin C | The selected commercial nutrition label does not provide an iron value, so the banana cannot be classified as an iron-rich food from this source. Its 9 mg vitamin C can support absorption of non-heme iron from other foods, so the overall result is kept neutral rather than favorable or less favorable. |
| Constipation / inadequate fibre intake | **Neutral** | Dietary fibre | The banana provides 1.4 g fibre per 100 g. Fibre contributes to normal bowel function, but this amount is modest relative to higher-fibre fruits and foods; therefore the classification is kept neutral within a simple nutrient-based decision-support framework. |

## 3. Interpretation Note

These classifications are intended for the Fruitelligence rule-based recommendation framework. They are nutrition-based decision-support outputs and do not diagnose, treat, or prevent disease.
