# Guava Nutritional Profile and Health-Based Mapping

**Basis:** Fresh guava. Values are per 100 g as reported on the commercial product page.

## 1. Nutritional Profile

| Nutrient / Feature | Value per 100 g |
|---|---:|
| Energy | 68 kcal |
| Carbohydrate | 14.3 g |
| Total sugars | 8.92 g |
| Dietary fibre | 5.4 g |
| Protein | 2.55 g |
| Total fat | 0.95 g |
| Calcium | 18 mg |
| Iron | 0.26 mg |
| Magnesium | 22 mg |
| Potassium | 417 mg |
| Sodium | 2 mg |
| Vitamin C | 228 mg |

**Commercial source:** https://varietyfoods.co.uk/products/brazilian-guava-500g

## 2. Final Health-Condition Mapping

| Health condition | Result | Main nutritional factors | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Diabetes / blood-glucose management | **Favorable** | Carbohydrate, total sugars, dietary fibre, energy | Guava provides 14.3 g carbohydrate and 8.92 g sugars per 100 g, together with 5.4 g dietary fibre and 68 kcal. The fibre content supports a more favourable whole-fruit profile than a sugar-only assessment. CDC notes that dietary fibre can help with blood-sugar control. |
| Hypertension / high blood pressure | **Favorable** | Potassium, sodium | The profile provides 417 mg potassium and only 2 mg sodium per 100 g. Potassium and lower sodium intake are recognized dietary factors that can support blood-pressure management. |
| Iron-deficiency anemia | **Neutral** | Iron, vitamin C | Guava contains 0.26 mg iron per 100 g, so it is not a major iron source. However, its very high vitamin C content (228 mg/100 g) can improve absorption of non-heme iron from other foods. Because the fruit itself is not iron-rich but may support iron absorption, the final classification is kept neutral. |
| Constipation / inadequate fibre intake | **Favorable** | Dietary fibre | Guava supplies 5.4 g dietary fibre per 100 g. This is a relatively high fibre content for a fruit, and adequate dietary fibre supports normal bowel function and can help prevent or manage constipation. |

## 3. Interpretation Note

These classifications are intended for the Fruitelligence rule-based recommendation framework. They are nutrition-based decision-support outputs and do not diagnose, treat, or prevent disease.
